일정은 아이폰과 맥에서도 보입니다Your events reach your iPhone and Mac

앱은 iPad 전용이지만 Boule Note에서 올린 일정은 아이패드의 기본 캘린더에 저장됩니다. 그 캘린더가 iCloud나 Google 계정 캘린더라면 아이폰, 맥, 애플 워치의 캘린더에도 그대로 표시됩니다. 반대로 아이폰에서 넣은 일정도 Boule Note의 월간·주간·일간 화면에 보입니다.

The app runs on iPad, but events you add in Boule Note are saved to the iPad’s built-in Calendar. If that calendar belongs to iCloud or Google, the events appear on your iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch too. Events you add on your iPhone show up in Boule Note’s month, week and day pages as well.

  1. Information collected: Boule Note does not collect or transmit any personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and no advertising or analytics tools.
  2. Information stored on the device: Handwriting, tags, text and notes are stored only on your iPad and are deleted when you delete the app.
  3. Calendar access: Only with your permission, the app reads iOS Calendar events to display them, and writes only the events you choose to add. Boule Note never sends calendar data to the developer or any other server. If the calendar you choose belongs to an account such as iCloud or Google, iPadOS syncs those events to your other devices according to that account’s settings. You can revoke access at any time in iPad Settings.
  4. Handwriting to text: Processed by Apple's on-device text recognition. No images or text are sent to any server.
  5. Backup files: Saved only to the location you choose.
